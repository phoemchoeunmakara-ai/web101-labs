Chicken Fried Rice

Easy Homemade Recipe

Chicken fried rice is a quick and tasty dish made with rice, chicken, vegetables, and eggs. It is perfect for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 cup chicken
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Salt and pepper

Cooking Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Cook the chicken until golden brown.
  3. Add eggs and vegetables.
  4. Put the cooked rice into the pan.
  5. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Mix everything well and serve hot.
